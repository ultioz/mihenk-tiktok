Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mihenk handles information when users connect their TikTok account or use Mihenk's publishing functionality.

1. Information We Process

When a TikTok account is connected, Mihenk may process information provided through TikTok's official APIs, including:

  • TikTok account identifiers
  • Authorization tokens
  • Account authorization status
  • Video publishing information
  • Post identifiers and publishing status
  • Technical logs required to operate the service

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

  • authenticate authorized TikTok accounts
  • publish requested video content
  • check publishing status
  • prevent duplicate publishing
  • maintain and troubleshoot the application

3. Data Sharing

Mihenk does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to TikTok and other technical service providers only when necessary to provide the application's publishing functionality.

4. Authentication Tokens

Authentication credentials and tokens are stored securely and are used only to communicate with authorized platform APIs. They are not intentionally exposed publicly.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the application, maintain publishing records, or comply with applicable requirements.

6. Revoking Access

Users may revoke Mihenk's access through TikTok account settings or other authorization controls provided by TikTok.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials, tokens, and application data from unauthorized access.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available on this page.
